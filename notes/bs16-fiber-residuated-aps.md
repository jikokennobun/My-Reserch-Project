## Core Idea

Reconstruct the cut elimination theorem of Beklemishev-Shamkanov 2016 (BS16) not by forcing it directly into ordinary APS, but by first interpreting it as a fibered, polarized, non-idempotent, residuated pre-APS.

The guiding slogan is:

$$
\text{BS16 cut elimination}
=
\text{closure of the cut-free fibered residuated APS under cut composition.}
$$

In this reading, ordinary APS appears only after adding a suitable diagonal contraction datum. The APS axiom A3 is not primitive from the resource-sensitive perspective; it hides a contraction-like principle.

## Minimal Algebraic Structure

A non-idempotent residuated APS should at least have

$$
\mathbb A =
(L,\le,\otimes,\mathbf 1,\backslash,/,\top,\bot,\Box,\boxtimes).
$$

The residuation laws are:

$$
a\otimes b\le c
\iff
a\le c/b
\iff
b\le a\backslash c.
$$

Implication is read as a residual, for example:

$$
a\to b := a\backslash b.
$$

Refutability is defined in the BS16 style by:

$$
\boxtimes A := \Box(A\to\bot).
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxtimes\top=\Box\bot.
$$

The formalized G2 principle becomes:

$$
\boxtimes\boxtimes\top\le\boxtimes\top,
$$

equivalently:

$$
\Box(\Box\bot\to\bot)\le\Box\bot.
$$

## A3 as Hidden Contraction

APS A3 has the form:

$$
x\le \Box y,\quad x\le \boxtimes y
\Longrightarrow
x\le \boxtimes\top.
$$

In a residuated/resource-sensitive setting, the two assumptions naturally combine as:

$$
x\otimes x\le \Box y\otimes\boxtimes y.
$$

To return from $x\otimes x$ to $x$, one needs a diagonal contraction-like principle:

$$
x\le x\otimes x.
$$

So A3 decomposes as:

$$
\text{provability/refutability collision} + \text{diagonal contraction}.
$$

This explains why BS16 can have Jeroslow-style fixed points while formalized G2 fails in the contraction-free setting.

## BS16 Separation

Take the Jeroslow fixed point:

$$
p := \operatorname{fp}x.\Box(x\to\bot).
$$

Then fixed point unfolding gives:

$$
p = \Box(p\to\bot)=\boxtimes p.
$$

But BS16 shows that the sequent

$$
\Box(\Box\bot\to\bot)\Rightarrow \Box\bot
$$

is not derivable. Algebraically:

$$
\exists p\,(p=\boxtimes p)
\not\Rightarrow
\boxtimes\boxtimes\top\le\boxtimes\top.
$$

This is a resource-sensitive separation between fixed point existence and formalized G2.

## Fibered Formulation

Use a base category of polarized contexts:

$$
\mathcal B =
\mathrm{MSet}(\mathrm{Fm})
\times
\mathrm{MSet}(\mathrm{Fm})^{op}.
$$

An object $(\Gamma,\Delta)$ represents the boundary of a sequent:

$$
\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta.
$$

The fiber

$$
\mathcal E_{\Gamma,\Delta}
$$

can be read as the preorder of cut-free proofs with boundary $\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta$, or as the algebraic proof values generated by that boundary.

Cut elimination becomes:

$$
\mathcal E^{\mathrm{cf}}\hookrightarrow\mathcal E
$$

is closed under cut composition.

That is, if

$$
\pi_1\in \mathcal E^{\mathrm{cf}}_{\Gamma;\Delta,A},
\qquad
\pi_2\in \mathcal E^{\mathrm{cf}}_{A,\Sigma;\Pi},
$$

then their cut composite normalizes to an element of

$$
\mathcal E^{\mathrm{cf}}_{\Gamma,\Sigma;\Pi,\Delta}.
$$

This is the fibered version of the BS16 cut admissibility theorem.

## Why Fibered?

BS16's modal rule separates active modal resources from passive weakening parameters. This is hard to see in a one-sorted algebra.

The modal rule has the schematic shape:

$$
\frac{\Sigma,\Box\Pi\Rightarrow A}
     {\Gamma,\Box\Sigma,\Box\Pi\Rightarrow\Box A,\Delta}.
$$

Here $\Box\Sigma,\Box\Pi$ are active modal resources, while $\Gamma,\Delta$ are passive parameters. A fibered structure tracks this boundary behavior explicitly.

## Link to Algebraic Proof Theory

The natural meta-theory is Ciabattoni-Galatos-Terui style algebraic proof theory:

$$
\text{cut elimination}
\quad\leftrightarrow\quad
\text{algebraic completion stability}.
$$

The expected extension is from FL/residuated lattices to modal fixed-point residuated APS structures.

Correspondence table:

| Algebraic proof theory | BS16 / Res-APS reading |

## Target Theorem Schema

For a BS16-style modal fixed-point substructural calculus $\mathcal L$, the following should correspond:

1. $\mathcal L$ has cut elimination.
2. The associated class of modal residuated APS structures is closed under an appropriate fibered MacNeille completion.
3. The canonical extensions of $\Box,\boxtimes,\operatorname{fp}$ preserve the relevant APS/G2 principles.
4. The difference between no contraction, restricted contraction, and full contraction is detected by A3, FG2, and Jeroslow fixed point behavior.
