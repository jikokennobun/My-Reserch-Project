### Abstract (source metadata)

For a given group $G$, it is natural to ask whether one can classify all isometric $G$-actions on Gromov hyperbolic spaces. We propose a formalization of this problem utilizing the complexity theory of Borel equivalence relations. In this paper, we focus on actions of general type, i.e., non-elementary actions without fixed points at infinity. Our main result is the following dichotomy: for every countable group $G$, either all general type actions of $G$ on hyperbolic spaces can be classified by an explicit invariant ranging in an infinite dimensional projective space or they are unclassifiable in a very strong sense.
