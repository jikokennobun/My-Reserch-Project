### Abstract (source metadata)

We construct an invariant symmetric ternary relation with existence and stationarity over models for classes of characteristic-zero Frobenius fields associated with Melnikov formations. For proper formations, the automorphism theories of their canonical root-predicate expansions have amalgamation and no model companion. The underlying fields are then unbounded, and their theories are NSOP$_1$ but not simple. In this case, on triples of elementary submodels, the constructed relation implies ordinary nonforking, which implies Kim-independence, which implies linear disjointness; all three implications are strict. We also study p.e.c. fields with group actions, namely fields existentially closed in every regular extension carrying an extension of the action. We prove that the class of p.e.c.\ difference fields of characteristic zero is not elementary, and that the theory of all characteristic-zero difference fields with canonical root predicates has no model companion.

### Abstract (source metadata)

This paper investigates a relational semantics for onto-epistemic logic with two semi-negations. The frames carry two pairs of quasi-orders and two commuting involutions. Some frames have nondistributive lattices of stable sets. The four- and nine-valued entailments are recovered as joint entailment on two particular finite frames. Sound and complete binary consequence calculi are given for both component entailments and for joint entailment. ### Abstract (source metadata)

We present a new axiomatic theory of disquotational truth based on the theory of positive disquotation PUTB. Formulated in classical logic, PUTB restricts the T-schema to positive sentences and satisfies key epistemic desiderata (semantic conservativity and trustworthiness). However, it fares poorly in proof-theoretic strength compared to reflective truth frameworks. To address this, we propose a new theory of positive disquotation aimed at axiomatizing its least fixed-point model. Without requiring additional resources, we achieve this by incorporating ill-founded reasoning under suitable soundness restrictions, applying the tools of cyclic proof theory to axiomatic theories of truth.
