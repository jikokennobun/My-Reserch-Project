### Abstract (source metadata)

We study the analytification and tropicalization of algebraic varieties over valued fields via Jun's spaces of hyperfield points. Our main new objects are a family of power-tropical hyperfields $H_n$ and their inverse limit $H$, recording at each level the class of a leading term modulo $n$-th powers of the residue field. They yield an enhanced tropicalization of the associated space $X(H)$, strictly finer than the Berkovich one exactly when the multiplicative group of the residue field is non-divisible: it specialises to the Berkovich analytification when the residue field is algebraically closed, to the real analytification of Jell-Scheiderer-Yu when it is real closed, and to a new $p$-adic analytification when it is $p$-adically closed. ### Abstract (source metadata)

We present Three-valued Fixpoint Quorum Logic, or 3muQL for short; a three-valued modal fixed-point logic designed for declaratively specifying and reasoning about distributed algorithms, such as the Paxos consensus algorithm. Our methodology represents a distributed algorithm as a logical theory, enabling correctness properties to be derived directly within the framework -- or revealing logical errors in the algorithm's design when they exist. 3muQL adopts a declarative approach, specifying the overall logic of computation without prescribing control flow. Notably, message-passing and state-transitions are not explicitly modelled, and notions of 'quorum' are abstracted from concrete thresholds to abstract modal operators. This distinguishes our framework from approaches like TLA+. The logical abstraction emphasises the essence of distributed algorithms, offering a novel perspective on their specification and reasoning. We define the syntax and semantics of 3muQL, explore its theoretical properties, and demonstrate its applicability through a detailed treatment of the Paxos consensus algorithm.
