### Abstract (source metadata)

Localic and realizability toposes are two central classes of toposes in categorical logic, both arising from the Hyland, Johnstone and Pitts tripos-to-topos construction. We investigate the common geometric structure underlying these classes by developing an algebraic abstraction of localic presheaves, localic sheafification, and the role of supercompactification in the Comparison Lemma. The resulting framework applies to a broad class of toposes arising from the tripos-to-topos construction, including all $\set$-based triposes. These results establish a common geometric framework encompassing both localic and realizability toposes.
